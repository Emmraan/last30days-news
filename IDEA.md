# last30days-new — Project Idea

**Concept:** An extensible, cross-platform, AI-powered News Aggregation, Social Media Monitoring and Research Platform.

**Vision:** One platform to discover, track, research and understand important developments happening across the world without switching between multiple news apps, websites and social media platforms.

## Core Features

### 1. Time-Based News Discovery
- Last 24 Hours
- Last 1 Day (calendar day)
- Last 7 Days
- Last 30 Days
- Custom Date Range

### 2. News Categories
- AI & Machine Learning
- Technology
- Politics
- Geopolitics
- Business & Economy
- Science & Space
- Cybersecurity
- Startups
- Custom Topics

Support global and country-specific news discovery.

### 3. Extensible Source Monitoring
A modular source system that allows adding new resources and integrations over time.

Potential sources:
- News websites and web search
- RSS feeds
- X/Twitter
- Reddit
- YouTube
- GitHub
- Hacker News
- arXiv
- Government and research publications
- Custom APIs and websites

**Individual Account Monitoring:** Users can track specific social media profiles, accounts, channels and communities. For example, when a tracked X account publishes a new post, it should automatically appear in the platform's feed with its original link, author and timestamp.

### 4. AI Research & Intelligence
AI should research collected developments and generate comprehensive, structured reports rather than simply summarizing headlines.

Expected capabilities:
- Multi-source research
- News summarization and analysis
- Duplicate detection
- Cross-source verification
- Important development ranking
- Timelines
- Different perspectives
- Impact analysis
- What's Next?
- Source citations
- Follow-up conversations about reports
- Compare different time periods

### 5. Flexible AI Configuration
Bring Your Own AI (BYOK) with optional hosted AI.

Potential providers:
- OpenAI
- Anthropic
- Google Gemini
- OpenRouter
- Groq
- DeepSeek
- Ollama
- LM Studio
- Custom OpenAI-compatible Base URL

Configuration: Base URL, API key (optional), model selection and AI enable/disable.

Basic news aggregation should work without AI credentials.

### 6. Web Application
A unified, responsive dashboard featuring:
- News feed
- Categories and time filters
- Social account monitoring
- Custom source manager
- Trending topics
- AI research reports
- Saved news and reports
- Search and discovery
- Notifications
- Dark and light themes

### 7. Installation & Deployment

**NPX:** `npx last30days-new`

NPX should install/run the application, start a local web server and open the Web App in the browser. The terminal is a launcher, not the primary news-reading interface.

Deployment possibilities:
- Local NPX installation
- Docker
- Self-hosted VPS
- Vercel-compatible deployment
- Future hosted SaaS

### 8. Product Philosophy
- Open and extensible source ecosystem.
- Cross-platform accessibility.
- AI-provider independence.
- Self-hosting flexibility.
- Reliable source attribution.
- Minimal information overload.
- Research-driven rather than headline-driven experience.

**Core Product Identity:**

last30days-new is not just another news aggregator. It aims to become a personal AI Intelligence Desk that continuously discovers, monitors, researches and explains the developments that matter.
